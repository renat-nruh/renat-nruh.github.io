Ore Empire Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

The short version

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat.
  • Your game save stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • If Share anonymous stats is on, the game sends game milestones, a frame-rate reading, error reports and a few technical details to our analytics provider, GameAnalytics, so we can fix problems and improve the game. These stats never include your name, email, contacts, precise location or advertising ID.
  • You can switch the stats off at any time in Settings (the ⚙️ button). To have stats already sent deleted, email us your Stats ID from the same screen.

1. Who we are

Ore Empire is a mining tycoon game made by an independent developer, published on Google Play under a personal developer account. In this policy, "we" means the Ore Empire developer. We decide how the data described here is used, which makes us the data controller (the "controlador" under Brazil's General Data Protection Law, the LGPD).

Contact: oreempireofficial@gmail.com

2. What the game collects

Data that stays on your phone

Your progress (the save) and your settings (sound, vibration, graphics, language and the stats switch) are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. The game plays offline.

The Save code in Settings is a piece of text that you copy yourself, for example to keep a backup. We only see it if you choose to send it to us.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the game's save in your phone's backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup under your account settings, and we can't access it.

Anonymous stats (only while "Share anonymous stats" is on)

The switch is on by default. While it is on, the game sends the following to GameAnalytics, and nothing else:

  • A random ID that the analytics software creates on your phone the first time it runs. It is not your Android advertising ID, it is not linked to your Google account, and it is the same for all the stats from this install. Settings shows it as your Stats ID.
  • Sessions: when each play session starts and ends (a session also ends when the game goes into the background), how long it lasted, and how many sessions this install has had.
  • Game milestones:
    • the first time you hit a rock, break one, sell, buy an upgrade, build a machine, open the second zone and sell a claim;
    • each machine you build (only its type: drill, conveyor, smelter or truck), each zone you open and each new stage you reach;
    • starting a claim (your first rock broken there) and selling it, with the number of deeds you earned;
    • collecting offline earnings, with how many minutes you were away;
    • the start of a Gold Rush;
    • tapping Copy on your save code (the code itself is never sent).
    Most milestones include your total play time so far.
  • Performance: at most one frame-rate reading each time the game runs, measured over about a minute of play: the average frames per second, and which range it falls in (under 20, 20–30, 30–45, 45–55, or 55 and over).
  • Errors: the text of up to 10 script errors each time the game runs (the error message, the name of the game file and the line number, at most 400 characters each), and a warning when the phone's graphics stop working (a "lost graphics context"). If the analytics software itself runs into a problem, it can report that too.
  • Technical details the analytics software adds to each message: a session ID, the time, your Android version (for example "android 14"), the name and version of the phone's built-in web engine, whether the phone is online, the analytics software's version and the game's version. Its device model and manufacturer fields are always sent as "unknown": it doesn't read them.
  • Country: GameAnalytics works it out from the IP address that every internet connection has. We never see or store your IP address.

We call these stats "anonymous" because they don't say who you are. They are still linked to the random ID, so we treat them as personal data and give you all the rights in section 7.

Some test builds of the game don't include analytics at all. In those builds the switch isn't shown and nothing is sent.

What we never collect

Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise (GPS) location, advertising ID, payment details or messages. The game has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat and no user-generated content.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply to you.

3. Why we use it

  • To see where players get stuck or stop playing, especially in the first minutes, so we can make the game clearer and more fun.
  • To find and fix crashes, errors and slow performance on different phones.
  • To decide what to improve or build next.

We don't use the stats for advertising or marketing profiles, and we never sell data.

4. Legal basis (LGPD)

For players in Brazil, we process the anonymous stats on the basis of our legitimate interest (LGPD, Article 7, IX) in keeping the game working and improving it. We collect only what that purpose needs, the stats can't identify you by name, and you can object at any time by switching them off. Where other privacy laws apply, such as the EU GDPR, we rely on the same legitimate interest.

5. Who processes the data

  • GameAnalytics receives and stores the stats on our behalf, as our data processor (the "operador" under the LGPD). Its servers may be outside Brazil, and it protects the data under its own data protection terms. See the GameAnalytics privacy policy.
  • Google Play distributes the game and gives us summary install statistics and crash reports, under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this web page. GitHub may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site uses no cookies or trackers.

We don't share data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Save and settings: on your phone until you reset your progress, clear the game's storage or uninstall the game.
  • Anonymous stats: only as long as they help us improve the game, and no longer than 2 years after they are collected. After that they are deleted or kept only as totals that can't be linked to any install.
  • Emails you send us: only as long as we need them to answer you.

7. Your choices and rights

Switch the stats off

Open Settings in the game (the ⚙️ button) and turn off Share anonymous stats. Sending stops immediately, and nothing is sent while it is off. The game works exactly the same either way. Switching off doesn't delete stats already sent: see below.

Delete your data

  • Stats already sent: open Settings and note the Stats ID shown under the stats switch (it appears once the game has sent stats). Email it to oreempireofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will have GameAnalytics delete everything filed under that ID and answer you within 15 days. Without the Stats ID we can't find your stats, because they aren't linked to your name, email, phone number or phone model.
  • On your phone: Reset progress in Settings deletes your game progress. To delete everything the game keeps on your phone, including the Stats ID, go to Android Settings > Apps > Ore Empire > Storage (or "Storage & cache") > Clear storage, or uninstall the game. After that, stats sent before can no longer be linked to your phone, so note your Stats ID first if you also want them deleted.

Your rights under the LGPD

You can ask us to confirm whether we process data about you, and to give you access to it, correct it, anonymize, block or delete it, or provide it in a portable format. You can also ask who we share data with, and object to the processing. We will answer within 15 days. You can also complain to Brazil's data protection authority, the ANPD.

8. Children

Ore Empire is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children. The game has no ads, no purchases, no chat and no loot boxes. Lucky Haul and Gold Rush are free in-game bonuses: they involve no real money and no betting. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child played with the stats switched on, you can turn the switch off and contact us to delete the data.

9. Security

Stats are sent only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your save is kept in the game's private storage on your phone. Our access to the analytics dashboard is protected by a password.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change is important, for example if the game starts collecting a new kind of data, we will also say so in the game's update notes on Google Play.
